## Historical Project Overview

Exohood Machine Learning was originally proposed as a research initiative exploring artificial intelligence, machine learning, software analysis, and automated development assistance.

Early project concepts included code analysis, security research, developer assistance, and the evaluation of machine learning techniques for software engineering.

These descriptions reflected research objectives and experimental proposals. They should not be interpreted as evidence that a fully autonomous artificial intelligence system was developed or deployed.

This initiative has been discontinued in its original form.

## Transition to Exania AI

Artificial intelligence research at Exohood Labs has evolved into **Exania AI**, a separate proprietary research and development initiative.

Exania AI focuses on selected areas of artificial intelligence, machine learning, computational analysis, and software engineering.

**Exania AI is not an open-source project.**

Its internal source code, models, algorithms, datasets, development tools, and technical infrastructure are proprietary and are not available for public access, modification, or redistribution unless explicitly released under separate terms.

The existence of this historical repository does not grant access to Exania AI technologies or imply that its internal development is publicly maintained.

## Contributions and Community Development

The former public contribution program is permanently closed.

Previously published contribution guidelines and development instructions are no longer applicable.
